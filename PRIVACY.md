# Privacy Policy

QueryCat does **not** collect, transmit or sell your personal data. There is no
telemetry, analytics or tracking: files, queries and results are processed locally,
and network requests happen only when you ask for them (installing plugins, querying
URLs, using an AI provider you configured, etc.).

Read the full policy in [docs/misc/privacy.md](docs/misc/privacy.md) or at
<https://querycat.readthedocs.io/en/latest/misc/privacy/>.
